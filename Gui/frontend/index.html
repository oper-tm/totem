Create a Google Apps Script
Estimated time: 2 minutes
1. Open script.new or script.google.com
https://script.google.com


2. Click on "New Project".

3. Copy this code.
Totem - Google Apps Script Relay Setup

4. Paste the code into the project you created.

5. Click the "deploy" button at the top right of the page.

6. Click on "New deployment".

7. On the page that opens, click on the gear icon.

8. Click on "Web app".

9. Set the "Who has access" to "Anyone".

10. Click on "Deploy".

If you don't see this page, ignore these steps and skip to step 15..
11. Click on "Authorise access".

12. Click on "Advanced".

13. Click on "Go to Untitled projected (unsafe)".
Note: This app is for Google itself, but an unknown bug is causing this security warning that doesn't matter.

14. Click on "Continue".

15. Now your AppScript key is ready, copy it and set it in the next step.
